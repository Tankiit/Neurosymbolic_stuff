# Working Notes

## Research position

This project is best framed as a study of learned control over reasoning operations rather than as a simple chain-of-thought enhancement.

## Paper-level tagline

> Existing reasoning systems can generate, revise, and search through candidate chains, but choosing among these operations is itself a sequential decision problem.

## Main contribution narrative

We investigate whether a learned controller can choose among reasoning actions from partial reasoning histories and improve the accuracy–computation tradeoff across mathematical, logical, and evidence-grounded reasoning tasks.

## Likely evaluation emphasis

- matched token budget,
- action-specific behavior,
- recovery vs damage,
- transfer across tasks and models.

## Long-term vision

The goal is not to replace reasoning with brute-force search, but to teach a model when to continue, revise, branch, or stop based on the structure of its own partial reasoning.
